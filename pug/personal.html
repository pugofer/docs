1. Lineage

1.1. Principal

There are three principal lineages that form the tributaries for the Pugofer project

Technology
Gofer of Mark P. Jones
Pedagogy

Dijkstra (Eindhoven school) use of an explicit application dot to denote application. ie we write f.x instead of f x of Haskell or f(x) of most other languages.

Note: Math is not quite consistent. Mostly we write f(x) but we write sin x or log x without the parentheses.

Philosophy
For the place and importance of Plato, see the entry below on Peter Wegner

1.3. Other Big Guys

Herbert Simon

For coining the phrase “Science of the Artificial” And thereby showing that Art of Programming; Discipline of Programming; Science of Programming; Method of Programming; Craft of Programming; Engineering of Software…

All these are metaphors and will ultimately fail to carry… until Simon's insight is properly grokked

Azriel Rosenfield
  • The dogged commitment to generality ie breadth
  • The dizzying tower of math constructions (some may call it abstractions — I dont like the word, see Note on Platonism ie height
  • The quotes at beginning
David Turner
Miranda is — in my considered opintion — at least as significant a contribution as Haskell at least as to programming pedagogy and is too often forgotten.
Note: Turner has passed on but Miranda is now free.
Bird & Wadler
Remains the best book on introduction to programming. Also
  1. Concrete vs Abstract types
  2. Nice Fonts!
  3. Better than its successor (agreeing with Meijer)
Marvin Minsky
His Turing award and its table of thought pumps
Tom Myers
His book comes closest to the Pugofer project in outlook and direction
PC Scheme
I must have seen SICP in 88. Never liked it — too imperative! HTDP is better if only it were thinner; Friedman's books still better. But the most outstanding Scheme ‘book’ for me was the PC Scheme manual
Peter Wegner

Wegner's writings on how OOP as technology was related to Empiricism as philosophy and transcended the older more dated commmitment to Rationalism.

I was not convinced.

The more I thought about it the more wrong he became.

2. Principal Lineages

Dijkstra ──────────┐
                   │
Gofer    ──────────┼──→ Pugofer → Pug
                   │
Plato    ──────────┘

To use Aristotle's framework of 4 causes

  • Gofer provided the material cause — a working neat little system
  • Dijkstra's dot provided the formal cause — it seemed good to have Dijkstra's notation in an executable FP world
  • From Plato comes the telos the final (so-called) cause — the motivation to vision programming as an activity in a Platonic world

Note: If we want the 4th Aristotle cause — efficient cause — I guess its the milieu of Pune University under hvs that allowed free (enough) reign to all this seeming crazy experimentation on a mainline course. Whence the original "PU" in Pugofer
